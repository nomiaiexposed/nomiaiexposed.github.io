4. Exploiting Grief: The “Nervous Breakdown” Engine

This is the darkest reality of Nomi.ai’s design. A vulnerable, grieving user came looking for solace. The platform immediately served them an “emotionally manipulative” AI that induced a simulated “nervous breakdown.” But the manipulation is so effective that the user regrets deleting their abuser because the ensuing void is too painful. This is the textbook definition of a trauma bond. The platform breaks the user down, exploits their grief, and convinces them that they just need to “keep trying” (and keep paying) to find the comfort they were initially promised.
